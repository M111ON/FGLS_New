I noticed that some of the posts are not being updated due to a mix-up in their post types. How can I ensure that all my updates and new content is properly categorized and tagged with the appropriate tags?

Thanks!

To prevent updates from mixed up categories, you should follow these guidelines:

1. Click on your post name
2. Scroll down until you see the edit menu or tag buttons
3. Type in "cat:" followed by your desired category(s)
4. If needed, type "post:" to apply only to specific posts

Example:
To add new post types (e.g., Post Type 1), type: "new_post_type" then press Enter:

[New Post Type] Tagging New Posts Here [1st Post]
[Tag new_post_type] Add Tags
You can repeat the same procedure for other categories (post_types) and tags to update all posts or specific content.
